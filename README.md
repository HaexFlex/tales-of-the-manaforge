# 🌳 Tales of the Manaforge

**Grow a living tree of mana. Forge legendary gear. Ascend, and try once more to save a broken realm.**

## 📜 The Legend

Long ago, the Ancient Keepers tended the Manatrees, the roots that bind the Manarealm together. Each of them swore to bring everlasting peace to the world.

None of them succeeded.

Again and again, their ambition ran deeper than their wisdom. They pulled too much from the roots, forged too much power and reached too high. And again and again, the Manarealm came close to tearing itself apart. All that's left of them are Runestones humming in the grass, echoes in forgotten chambers, and a single sapling in a quiet clearing.

Now that sapling is yours. **You are the new Keeper.** Will you break the cycle, or become one more echo in the Manaforge?

## 🌱 Your clearing

Deep in an ancient forest, the Manatree waits. You tend it from a fragile sapling to a towering Ancient, gather wood, stone and berries, and command a little swarm of glowing Wisps who work for you even while you're away.

When the tree finally bears its **Primordial Fruit**, you choose what power to carry into your next life, then **Ascend** and start over with everything you've learned. Every cycle brings you a little closer to what the Ancient Keepers never managed to do.

## ✨ What you'll do

- 🌱 **Grow the Manatree** through five stages, from Sapling to Ancient. Each stage makes your harvest stronger.
- 🧚 **Command Wisps.** Drag-select them, send them to gather, and watch them follow you around the clearing.
- 🔨 **Walk into the Forge** and run five crafting stations, including the Anvil, the Crucible and the Mill. Turn raw materials into weapons and relics.
- 💎 **Spend Manashards wisely.** Spend them now on Runestone power, or save them for a bigger Ascension? That's up to you.
- 🌀 **Explore the Echo Chamber** and meet Elaia, who remembers more than she lets on.
- 🌙 **Idle-friendly.** Your clearing keeps working while you're gone, and checking in often pays off.

All of it comes in a hand-crafted **SNES-inspired pixel-art** look, with chiptune-flavoured sound.

## 👨‍👧‍👦 About this project

Tales of the Manaforge is a **hobby game made by a dad of three**, built in the precious hours after the kids are asleep.

It's also **fully vibe-coded**: every line of code, every sprite and every sound comes from working with AI assistants. I describe the game I want to play, and a little team of AI helpers builds it with me. It's an experiment in how far a busy parent with big ideas can get.

Built with [Godot 4](https://godotengine.org/).
