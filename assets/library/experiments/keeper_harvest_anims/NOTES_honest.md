# Keeper harvest clips (Imagine key poses): honest notes

- **What worked:** reading and timing. All 7 clips (chop S/E/W, pluck S/E/W, mine S) are 6 frames, 1000 ms, on 192x128 with the feet at (96,128). The READY frame stands 122 px tall, the sole is on row 123 in every frame, and the strike frame is in each meta json (`strike_at_ms`). Pluck is the cleanest: the body stays at 122 px and the feet don't move.
- **Style match vs the painted walk: weak.** Imagine draws crisper, more saturated pixel art: hard outlines, a shorter coat, bigger boots and spikier hair. The walk is softer and painterly. Head drift vs idle is 45-60 for every pose; live walk frames score 1-35. This gap belongs to the whole set, not to a few bad poses. The palette snap does almost nothing because the Keeper "palette" is the walk's ~25k unquantised colours. A tight 32/64-colour snap turned the steel gold, so I dropped it.
- **Chunkiness:** the Imagine Keeper was 15-20% wider at the coat and hips. I squeezed every key to 0.88 width about the feet, which brings the build close but leaves the head about 2-3 px narrower than the walk's. Trousers were greyer, not really darker (saturation 0.5-0.75 vs 0.87). I pushed them toward the walk's warm brown; that's better, not exact.
- **Flicker:** every key is a fresh painting, so hair spikes and coat folds change between frames. Same-sheet head drift is 10-22 on east sheets and 28-48 on south sheets. The walk's own frame-to-frame figure is 24-44, so it's tolerable at 100-200 ms holds but visible in the comparison GIFs. Only 4 keys exist (3 for mine), so frames are doubled rather than in-betweened. My rig's in-betweens would not match Imagine's rendering.
- **Tool size:** the east axe is a big double-bit axe, about 1.8x the south axe's metal area, and the south axe and pick are a bit large next to the walk's proportions. East and south chops don't read as the same tool.
- **Frame fit:** 128 px of height is too short for overhead swings. Chop wind-ups overflowed by 5 px (S) and 4 px (E); I rotated the axe 18 and 28 degrees about the grip to fit. The mine wind-up overflowed by 25 px and was REJECTED, so mine is a 3-key loop that uses READY (pick on shoulder) as its wind-up. Mine's follow-through loses 1 px of pick tip. 192 px width was enough everywhere.
- **FX:** chips, berry pop and sparks are procedural and flat. They read at x2 but are plainer than the chips Imagine painted itself, and wood chips get lost on bark.
- **West** is a mirror of east, so the coat drape and the axe hand flip and the feet sit 1 px off centre.
- **What would fix it:** (1) Get Imagine to repaint the WALK and idle in the same pass as the harvest keys. One art style beats matching two. (2) Or use a style-reference/img2img pass with the native 170x256 walk frame as the reference, plus a fixed tool sprite pasted in afterwards. (3) Ask for 6 keys per clip with the tool kept below head height (or allow a 160 px tall canvas) for overhead swings. (4) Build a real ~48-colour Keeper palette and snap both walk and harvest to it.
